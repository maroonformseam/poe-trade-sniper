FAQ

Questions from exiles

Everything you need to know before you start sniping the trade site.

Is poe-trade-sniper against Path of Exile's terms of service?+
poe-trade-sniper only reads the public trade API that the official trade site already exposes, at respectful polling rates. It does not automate gameplay, move your character, or interact with the game client — you always send whispers yourself.
How fast are the whisper alerts?+
Alerts fire as soon as the next poll cycle returns a new matching listing. Polling intervals adapt to the API's rate limits, so you get the fastest safe cadence without getting throttled or temporarily banned.
Can I use my own loot filters and price rules?+
Yes. Filters are fully custom: item names, base types, mods, rarity, price ceilings and flip margins. You can keep multiple filter profiles per league and toggle them from the tray menu.
